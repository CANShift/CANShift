Direction 2a — Bracket
CANSHIFT
Construction

One 13-unit stroke, butt terminals cut on the vertical, right angles only, 11° shear. The same rule builds the C and the S, so the pair locks up with no optical correction. The rule under the wordmark is a CAN frame rhythm — it is part of the mark, never decoration added elsewhere.

Do
Keep the shear at 11° — the mark and the wordmark share it.
Clear space on all sides equals the stroke width of the C.
On dark grounds the C is paper, the S stays red.
Don't
No gradient, no chrome bevel, no drop shadow — that is the old mark.
No outline version, no rounded terminals, no second red.
Never set the wordmark in another typeface or unshear it.

Archivo 400 — body copy. Interface labels are 800 in small caps tracking, 0.18em to 0.22em, never below 9 px.
Monospace — every number the car produces
Values are always monospaced and tabular — digits must not shift as they change.
Labels above the value, never beside it.
Units at a quarter of the value size, in dim.
Minimum on the panel: 30 px value at 2×, 10 px label.

Layout rules
12-column grid, 12 px gutter. Widgets span columns, never fixed pixels.
Primary values carry a 2 px ink rule; secondaries a 1 px track rule; danger a 2 px red rule.
Touch targets never below 48 × 50 px real. Buttons are outlined, filled red when engaged.
Anything outside 320 × 240 is flagged in the Tuner, not silently scaled.
Motion
Rev limit — hard on-off at 6 Hz, no easing.
Armed states — 1 s ease-in-out pulse, 100 % to 35 %.
Values snap, never tween. Bars catch up in 120 ms linear.
Stale after 500 ms without a frame: grey and - -.
Page change is an instant cut — nothing for the eye to track.
